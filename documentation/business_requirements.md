
# Business Requirements

## 1. Project Title

Telecom Customer Churn Prediction and Retention Optimization

## 2. Industry

Telecommunications

## 3. Business Background

Telecommunication companies face customer loss due to
service quality issues, customer dissatisfaction,
competition and changing customer behaviour.

Understanding customer churn and identifying high-risk
customers can help companies improve customer retention
and allocate marketing resources effectively.

## 4. Business Problem

The company needs to identify customers who are likely
to discontinue its services, understand factors
associated with churn and develop effective customer
retention strategies within a limited budget.

## 5. Project Objectives

- Identify customer churn patterns.
- Investigate factors associated with customer churn.
- Analyse customer behaviour and service usage.
- Perform statistical hypothesis testing.
- Develop machine learning models to predict churn.
- Segment customers based on churn risk and value.
- Develop a retention budget optimization simulator.
- Create interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Provide data-driven business recommendations.

## 6. Target Stakeholders

- Business Management
- Marketing Department
- Customer Retention Department
- Customer Experience Team
- Business Intelligence Team

## 7. Business Questions

1. What is the overall customer churn rate?
2. Which customer segments have the highest churn rate?
3. Are customer complaints associated with churn?
4. Are call failures associated with customer churn?
5. How does subscription length relate to churn?
6. Which tariff plans have higher churn rates?
7. How does service usage relate to churn?
8. Which factors are most useful for predicting churn?
9. Which high-value customers are at risk of leaving?
10. How can the retention budget be allocated effectively?

## 8. Key Performance Indicators

### Total Customers
Number of customer records in the analytical dataset.

### Churned Customers
Number of customers with a churn status of 1.

### Non-Churned Customers
Number of customers with a churn status of 0.

### Customer Churn Rate
Churned Customers / Total Customers * 100

### Customer Retention Rate
Non-Churned Customers / Total Customers * 100

### Customer Complaint Rate
Customers with Complaints / Total Customers * 100

### Average Subscription Length
Average number of subscription months.

### Average Customer Value
Mean of the dataset's calculated customer value.

### High-Risk Customers
Customers whose predicted churn probabilities exceed
a threshold selected during model validation.

### Customer Value at Risk
Sum of predicted churn probability multiplied by
the calculated customer value for eligible customers.

### Expected Retention Net Benefit
Simulated retention benefit minus campaign cost,
based on explicitly documented business assumptions.

## 12. Project Limitations

- The dataset originates from an Iranian telecom company,
  not a Sri Lankan telecommunications company.
- Customer value is not confirmed monetary revenue.
- Actual retention campaign costs and outcomes are unavailable.
- Financial results will be based on simulated assumptions.
- Statistical associations do not establish causation.
- Model predictions estimate risk rather than guarantee outcomes.

## 13. Success Criteria

- Produce accurate and validated business KPIs.
- Identify meaningful factors associated with churn.
- Evaluate predictive models using unseen test data.
- Compare retention strategies under consistent assumptions.
- Deliver reproducible analysis and interactive dashboards.
- Provide practical business recommendations supported by data.
